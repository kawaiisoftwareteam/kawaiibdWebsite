About Kawaii Group

Japan · Bangladesh · Opportunity

Building bridges between people, business, and culture

Kawaii Group was founded with one core idea: to create opportunities. Whether it is helping businesses thrive or guiding individuals toward new career paths, we are passionate about making a meaningful difference. With roots in both Japan and Bangladesh, our journey over the past decades has been about fostering connections — between countries, companies, and communities.

Through innovation, education, and strategic partnerships, we support manufacturing excellence, international recruitment, Japanese language learning, technology solutions, and global trade. Our sister concerns work together so clients can access specialized expertise under one trusted group identity.

From Dhaka to Tokyo and beyond, our teams remain focused on quality, transparency, and customer success. Explore our businesses, learn our story, and connect with us when you are ready to grow with a partner who builds bridges and creates smiles.

Common Questions About Kawaii Group

Everything you need to know about our Japan-Bangladesh joint ventures, talent placement, BIM education, and corporate services.

Kawaii Group provides comprehensive Japan-Bangladesh business solutions, including Japan Career & HR Solutions (SSW and TITP training and placement), Japanese Language Education (JLPT N5-N1), BIM & Construction DX Training and Consulting, Cross-border Business Consultancy, and Global Trading and Garments sourcing.

We offer end-to-end career transition programs including intensive Japanese language courses, Japanese corporate etiquette training, technical skill matching, visa documentation (Specified Skilled Worker - SSW, Engineering/Specialist in Humanities), and post-arrival settlement support in Japan through our Tokyo office.

Our BIM (Building Information Modeling) and Construction Digital Transformation (DX) programs provide hands-on training led by Japanese AEC industry experts (such as Dr. Shunsuke Someya). Participants gain industry-ready skills conforming to Japanese and international construction standards.

Our global headquarters is in Dhaka, Bangladesh (Suite-2A, House # 11, Block-B, Main Road, Banasree, Rampura). We also maintain branch offices in Tokyo (Japan), Montreal (Canada), Großbeeren (Germany), and Kuala Lumpur (Malaysia).
