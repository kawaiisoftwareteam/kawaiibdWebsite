The gap after language class

Language done. Interview still feels uncertain.

Many students complete JLPT N5 / N4 or JFT A1 / A2 successfully — yet feel nervous when a real Japanese interview approaches.

  • How do I introduce myself in Japanese?
  • How should I answer the interviewer?
  • What kinds of questions will come?
  • How do I speak confidently in front of a Japanese interviewer?

The opportunity

Practice before the real interview

Kawaii Group, in collaboration with Kawaii Group Japan, is hosting a special Japanese Practical Interview for N5 and N4 completed students.

This is not practice with a Bangladeshi trainer alone — you will experience an interview environment closer to the real thing, with an experienced native Japanese interviewer.

  • Free Japanese practical interview
  • Real interview-style practice with a Japanese interviewer
  • Build communication, confidence, and response skills
  • Pathway to upcoming real recruitment opportunities
Interviewed by a native Japanese interviewer

Your path with Kawaii Group

If you qualify through the practical interview, our team guides you through the next steps of your Japan journey.

  2. 02

    Move closer to your goal with Kawaii Group beside you.

Who can join

Built for N5 & N4 completed students

If you have finished Japanese language training and feel unsure about the next interview step — this opportunity is for you.

  • JLPT N5 or N4 completed (or equivalent JFT A1 / A2)
  • Students currently finishing their Japanese course
  • Anyone nervous about COE / company interview preparation
  • Candidates aiming for upcoming Japanese company recruitment
